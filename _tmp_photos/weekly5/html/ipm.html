    Dr Iveta Garaiova

    Scientist, ProVen Biotics, UK

    Iveta Garaiova obtained her PhD in Biochemistry at Comenius University in Bratislava, Slovakia, where she also worked as a research scientist at the Faculty of Medicine. She joined the company in 2003, focusing on probiotics and their impact on well-being. Iveta is a lead author or a co-author of numerous peer reviewed publications and provides scientific support for ProVen Biotics. She is also a lay member of the local research ethics committee, WALES REC 6.
